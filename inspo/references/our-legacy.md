# Our Legacy — runway poster

https://www.ourlegacy.com/ · observed 2026-10-28 · shots: `ourlegacy_1.jpg`, `ourlegacy_2.jpg`

**Feeling:** confident, a little cold, very modern.

- **Layout:** a 50/50 split the height of the screen: left half off-white with type,
  right half a full-height runway photo on black.
- **Type:** huge heavy grotesk caps ("OUR LEGACY", "MENS") with a lighter,
  regular-weight line indented beneath ("New Arrivals"). Two weights, one family,
  the contrast doing all the work.
- **Navigation:** just icons and a hamburger; the type is the navigation.
- **Colour:** off-white, black, and whatever the photograph brings.
- **Borrow:** the split screen; heavy caps + light indented subline; black photo panels.

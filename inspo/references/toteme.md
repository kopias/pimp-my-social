# Toteme — quiet luxury

https://toteme.com/ · observed 2026-10-28 · shots: `toteme_1.jpg`, `toteme_2.jpg`

**Feeling:** expensive because almost nothing is there.

- **Header:** wordmark centred in plain uppercase grotesk, small. Utility links
  ("Search", "Account") in tiny text on the right; icons are hairline.
- **Hero:** not full-bleed. One portrait-format photograph, about a quarter of the
  page width, centred in white. A two-line caption under it in the smallest size:
  the collection name, then a status line ("Coming soon").
- **Scroll:** images change scale and position as you go: a wide triptych crop, then
  a single small portrait set off-centre. White space is the layout.
- **Colour:** white, black, greys from the photos. No accent at all.
- **Type:** one grotesk. Everything under 14px except nothing; scale comes from
  images, not headlines.
- **Borrow:** the small centred image + whisper caption; the refusal to fill space.
- **Avoid copying:** their wordmark and photos.
